## What does a deep clean include?

A deep clean gives more time to buildup and detail across an occupied home. It normally covers the same core rooms as a [Standard House Clean](/services/standard-clean/), but with closer attention to edges, skirting, fittings and residue that routine maintenance cannot realistically address in the same visit.

It does not automatically include every specialist task. At Maid At Home, inside ovens, fridges, windows, balconies, targeted walls, wet-wiping blinds, empty cabinet interiors and carpet steam cleaning are selected separately unless another service says they are included.

Use the service's published checklist as the contract for scope. “Deep clean” is not a universal industry definition.

## Before starting the deep clean

Preparation makes the difference between cleaning surfaces and spending the day relocating objects. Complete these steps first:

Photograph damage and disclose natural stone, unsealed timber or product restrictions. Never mix cleaning chemicals or use an unknown product across a full surface.

For a professional visit, follow the full guide on [how to prepare before the cleaner arrives](/blog/how-to-prepare-for-house-cleaner/).

## Work in the right order

Clean from high to low so loosened dust lands on surfaces that have not yet been completed. Begin in drier rooms, move through the kitchen and bathrooms, and leave vacuuming and mopping until the end.

A practical sequence is:

1. remove clutter and rubbish;
2. dry dust high, reachable surfaces;
3. clean bedrooms and living areas;
4. degrease the kitchen;
5. apply bathroom products and allow safe dwell time;
6. wipe edges, skirting and contact points;
7. vacuum all accessible floors;
8. mop suitable hard floors;
9. inspect in daylight after surfaces dry.

Do not create an impossible checklist by adding appliance interiors, every window and full carpet treatment without allowing more time.

## Kitchen deep cleaning checklist

Focus on ordinary accessible buildup rather than dismantling appliances or attempting repairs.

- Remove loose crumbs and dry debris.
- Degrease benchtops and accessible splashback.
- Clean the sink, tapware and drain area.
- Attend the cooktop exterior and surrounding surfaces.
- Wipe appliance exteriors and handles.
- Clean outside cupboard and drawer surfaces.
- Wipe reachable rangehood exterior and safe accessible detail.
- Attend skirting, ledges and contact points.
- Vacuum and mop the floor last.

Inside-oven cleaning is a separate detailed task for Standard and Deep services. If it is selected, follow the appliance instructions and allow enough time for racks, trays, cavity and door glass. The [Melbourne oven cleaning guide](/blog/oven-cleaning-melbourne/) explains cost and scope.

Thick carbon, damaged coatings and sealed components cannot be restored by ordinary cleaning.

## Bathroom deep cleaning checklist

Bathrooms often need product dwell time for soap residue and mineral buildup. Ventilate the room and use products compatible with the surface.

- Clean the shower or bath and accessible screen.
- Attend tiles, ledges and fittings within safe reach.
- Clean the toilet, including accessible hinges and exterior detail.
- Clean vanity, basin, mirrors and tapware.
- Wipe outside cupboard surfaces and handles.
- Attend reachable vents, skirting and contact points.
- Vacuum and mop the floor.

Cleaning is not mould remediation, regrouting or silicone replacement. Persistent black marks, damaged sealant or moisture problems require proper assessment rather than stronger fragrance or repeated scrubbing.

## Bedrooms and wardrobes

Begin with reachable high dust, then move to horizontal surfaces, bed frames and floors. Clear bedside tables and dressers so they can be cleaned rather than worked around.

- Dust accessible ledges, frames and fittings.
- Wipe switches and safe contact points.
- Attend skirting and visible edges.
- Clean reachable mirrors.
- Vacuum under light, safely movable items where agreed.
- Vacuum carpet or mop suitable hard flooring.

Wardrobe exteriors can be part of whole-home detail. Empty wardrobe interiors take additional time and should be explicitly selected rather than assumed.

## Living areas, entries and stairs

Prioritise the surfaces and routes that shape the room visually. Dust shelves, tables, ledges, skirting and accessible furniture edges. Vacuum sofa edges only if the scope includes upholstery work and the material is suitable.

Entries often collect tracked dirt, pet hair and outdoor debris. Clean the interior door, handles, visible ledges and floor. On stairs, work from the top down and keep the route safe while products or floors dry.

Heavy furniture should not be moved casually. Clean the accessible area and agree separately on any safe furniture movement.

## Floors and carpet

Vacuum after all higher dusting is finished. Use the correct method for timber, tile, vinyl, laminate or stone; too much water or the wrong chemical can damage a floor even when the intention is cleaning.

Routine vacuuming removes loose surface debris. It does not replace [Carpet Steam Cleaning](/services/carpet-clean/), stain treatment or restoration. Steam-cleaned carpet normally needs **6–12 hours** to dry, depending on fibre, weather, soil and airflow. Plan with the [carpet cleaning time and drying guide](/blog/how-long-does-carpet-cleaning-take-melbourne/).

Permanent stains, worn coatings, scratches and water damage are not cleaning failures.

## Optional deep-cleaning extras

Use extras to solve specific needs instead of turning every deep clean into an unlimited project.

| Extra | When it is useful |
| --- | --- |
| Inside oven | Baked grease or a neglected appliance interior |
| Inside fridge | Empty appliance before restocking food |
| Interior windows | Fingerprints, dust and accessible internal glass |
| Empty cabinets | Before reorganising or moving items back |
| Wet-wipe blinds | Detailed slat cleaning where material allows |
| Balcony | Accessible outdoor dust and debris |
| Carpet steam | Embedded soil, odour or move-related requirements |

## How long does a deep clean take?

There is no reliable answer based on bedrooms alone. Bathrooms, living areas, levels, clutter, pets and buildup can change the work dramatically. Many homes need roughly four to eight team hours, but that is guidance rather than a completion promise.

Compare the service level in [Standard versus Deep Cleaning](/blog/standard-vs-deep-cleaning/). If only a short ordered task list matters, [Hourly Cleaning](/services/hourly-clean/) may be more suitable than a whole-home deep scope.

## When should you book a professional deep clean?

Professional help is useful when buildup appears across several rooms, a deadline is fixed, safe equipment is needed or the home has become too large to reset in one realistic DIY session. It can also establish a baseline before weekly or fortnightly maintenance begins.

A professional service still has limits. Hazardous material, hoarding remediation, pest treatment, unsafe-height work, rubbish removal and repairs require specialist support.
