## Standard, Deep or Hourly cleaning?

A [Standard House Clean](/services/standard-clean/) suits a maintained, lived-in apartment needing routine kitchen, bathroom, surface and floor care. It offers a defined scope and bedroom-based total.

A [Deep Clean](/services/deep-clean/) is the better start when grease, soap residue, edge dust or neglected detail appears across the apartment. Booking Standard Cleaning while expecting a deep reset creates a scope mismatch.

[Hourly Cleaning](/services/hourly-clean/) costs $60 per booked team hour with a two-hour minimum. Two cleaners working for one hour use two team hours. It suits a priority list when tasks beyond the booked time may remain.

## What changes the apartment cleaning cost?

### Bedrooms and condition

Bedroom count sets the flat-rate base because it approximates the whole-home scope. Bathrooms, living spaces, kitchen and floors remain part of the property. Routine marks fit Standard Cleaning; widespread buildup may require Deep Cleaning.

### Optional extras

Current optional prices include:

Carpet steam cleaning is priced separately according to room quantities. Review [Carpet Cleaning](/services/carpet-clean/) if vacuuming is not enough.

### Frequency

Eligible weekly and fortnightly Standard Cleaning receives 10% off from the second service. Eligible cleaning every three or four weeks receives 5% off from the second service. The first visit remains at the one-off rate.

## Apartment access and booking time

Share parking, loading, lift, concierge, key and building-entry instructions before the service. Restricted access does not automatically change the published bedroom price, but delays reduce usable time and may affect equipment arrangements.

Tell the team about:

- visitor parking or loading restrictions;
- lift bookings and floor number;
- concierge sign-in;
- lockbox or key collection;
- building rules for waste or equipment;
- pets inside the apartment.

The [preparing for a cleaner guide](/blog/how-to-prepare-for-house-cleaner/) provides a complete arrival checklist.

## What is included?

Core cleaning covers accessible kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living and floor surfaces according to the selected service. It does not automatically include every appliance interior, cabinet, window or specialist carpet treatment.

Read [what is included in a Melbourne house clean](/blog/what-is-included-in-house-clean/) before comparing quotes. A cheaper figure is not better value when it excludes expected tasks.

## How to reduce cost without reducing the result

Choose the correct service, clear loose belongings and select extras deliberately. More of the appointment can be used for cleaning when access is simple and surfaces are available.

Recurring service can reduce eligible Standard prices, but frequency should match actual use. Compare [weekly and fortnightly cleaning](/blog/weekly-vs-fortnightly-house-cleaning/) instead of choosing frequency only for the discount.

Do not understate condition to obtain a lower starting quote. A realistic scope is more useful than a cheap booking that cannot deliver the expected result.
