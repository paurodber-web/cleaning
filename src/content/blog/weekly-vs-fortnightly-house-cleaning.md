## Weekly and fortnightly cleaning compared

Weekly cleaning resets the core rooms every seven days. Fortnightly cleaning leaves two weeks between visits and assumes the household will manage spills, clutter and some routine work in between.

Neither frequency is automatically better. A one-bedroom apartment occupied by one person can need less help than a three-bedroom home with children, pets and daily cooking. Choose by how quickly the home loses its baseline.

| Factor | Weekly | Fortnightly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best fit | High-use households | Moderate household use |
| Between-visit work | Light | Some routine upkeep needed |
| Floors and pet hair | Reset more often | May build up before visit two |
| Kitchens and bathrooms | Less time for residue to accumulate | Works when occupants wipe between visits |
| Eligible Maid At Home saving | 10% from service two | 10% from service two |

## Choose weekly cleaning when use returns quickly

Weekly [Regular House Cleaning](/services/regular-house-cleaning/) can suit households where the same tasks become visible again within a few days. Common examples include young children, multiple pets, shared homes, frequent cooking, several bathrooms or residents with limited time for physical cleaning.

The benefit is not only appearance. Shorter intervals make the next visit more predictable because ordinary dust, floor debris and bathroom use have less time to accumulate. That consistency lets each service focus on maintaining the agreed baseline rather than recovering it.

Weekly cleaning does not replace daily household responsibilities. Spills, food safety, clutter, pet accidents and urgent hygiene issues still need prompt attention between professional visits.

## Choose fortnightly cleaning for balanced support

Fortnightly service is a practical starting point for many Melbourne homes. It can cover routine kitchens, bathrooms, dusting and floors while residents handle light wiping, dishes, spills and basic tidying during the alternate week.

It often fits couples, small families, maintained apartments and households that want weekends back without outsourcing every part of home care. If the cleaner repeatedly faces heavy floor debris, thick bathroom marks or more work than the defined scope can hold, the interval may be too long—or the home may need a Deep Clean before regular maintenance continues.

## Five questions that decide the frequency

1. **How fast do floors show use?** Pets, outdoor activity and busy entries can make seven days more realistic than fourteen.
2. **How often do you cook?** Daily cooking can return grease and surface work quickly.
3. **How many bathrooms are in regular use?** More users generally shorten the comfortable interval.
4. **What will residents do between visits?** Fortnightly works better when someone handles spills, clutter and light maintenance.
5. **What standard do you want to maintain?** A consistently guest-ready home needs a different rhythm from occasional support.

## Can you clean every three or four weeks?

Yes. Maid At Home's eligible three- and four-week recurring services receive 5% off from the second visit. Longer intervals can suit smaller, low-use homes where occupants actively maintain kitchens, bathrooms and floors.

They are less suitable if every visit begins with significant buildup. In that situation, booking less often does not necessarily save money: the home may require a deeper service or more hours to recover the expected standard.

## Start with a trial and adjust

If you are unsure, begin fortnightly and evaluate after two or three completed cleans. Notice what the home looks like in the final days before each visit, which tasks you perform between appointments and whether the cleaner can complete the agreed regular scope without rushing.

Move to weekly if the baseline disappears too early. Extend the interval if the home remains comfortably maintained and you are paying for work that does not yet need repeating. Discuss schedule changes early because preferred days depend on route availability.
