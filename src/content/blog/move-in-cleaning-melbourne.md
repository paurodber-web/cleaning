## What should a move-in clean include?

A move-in clean prepares an empty property for everyday use before belongings make access more difficult. It should cover the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, empty storage and floors, with any specialist work agreed separately.

The exact scope depends on whether you are buying, renting or moving into a newly renovated property. A [professional Move-In Clean](/services/move-in-clean/) is designed for an empty home; it is different from a [Standard Clean](/services/standard-clean/) for a maintained, occupied property.

A move-in service can remove accessible dust, residue and ordinary previous use. It cannot repair damage, remove hazardous material, remediate mould, control pests or guarantee that every permanent mark will disappear.

## Inspect and photograph before cleaning

Before changing the condition of the property, walk through in daylight and photograph every room. Record existing marks, damaged finishes, appliance condition, pests, mould concerns and anything that may need repair rather than cleaning.

For a Victorian rental, compare the home with the entry condition report and submit corrections within the required process before cleaning alters the evidence. Consumer Affairs Victoria explains how renters and rental providers should use [condition reports](https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/housing/renting/rent-bond-bills-and-condition-reports/condition-reports). This article is practical cleaning guidance, not tenancy advice.

If you purchased the home, keep photographs from the final inspection and raise settlement or property-condition concerns with the appropriate professional. A cleaner should not be asked to decide who is responsible for existing damage.

## Choose the right day for the clean

The ideal sequence is repairs, painting and trade work first; cleaning second; movers and furniture last. If cleaners work while trades are sanding, drilling or walking through rooms, fresh dust can settle on completed surfaces.

Confirm that electricity, lighting and hot water work. Provide authorised access, keys, parking information and any lift reservation. Empty-property cleaning still needs safe utilities and enough time before movers arrive.

## Clean empty cupboards and storage first

Start inside kitchen cupboards, wardrobes, drawers and other empty storage so clean belongings can be unpacked directly. Work from the highest shelf down and finish each unit before moving to exterior doors and handles.

Check for loose liners, crumbs, pest evidence, moisture and damaged coatings. Cleaning can remove ordinary accessible residue; it does not repair swollen cabinetry, peeling surfaces or active infestation.

Do not fill a cupboard until it is completely dry. Photograph any concern that becomes visible only after shelves are emptied.

## Move-in kitchen checklist

The kitchen is easier to reset before food and appliances are unpacked. Work through:

- empty cupboard and drawer interiors and exteriors;
- benchtops, splashback, sink and tapware;
- cooktop and accessible rangehood surfaces;
- oven interior, racks and door glass where included;
- fridge interior if the appliance remains and the task is selected;
- appliance exteriors and handles;
- skirting, reachable ledges and accessible wall marks;
- vacuuming and mopping after all higher work is complete.

Check that the oven and fridge are safe and powered according to their instructions before cleaning. A cleaner should not dismantle sealed components or repair faulty appliances.

If you plan to stock food immediately, allow shelves and the fridge interior to dry and return to a safe operating temperature first.

## Move-in bathroom and laundry checklist

Clean the shower or bath, screen, accessible tiles, toilet, vanity, basin, mirrors, taps, laundry trough, cupboards and floors. Pay attention to handles and other high-contact points, but assess grout, silicone and ventilation separately.

Black marks, damaged sealant or persistent odour may indicate a maintenance or mould issue that ordinary domestic cleaning cannot solve. Renters should document and report concerns through the proper channel instead of masking them with fragrance or paint.

Confirm that taps drain correctly and that visible leaks are reported. Cleaning is not plumbing repair.

## Bedrooms and living areas

Dust accessible surfaces, wardrobes, ledges, skirting and fittings from high to low. Remove safe reachable cobwebs, clean selected interior glass and attend visible door and handle marks within scope.

This is the best time to reach edges that furniture will soon block. However, unsafe-height work, exterior windows and renovation dust may need specialist assessment.

If the home has natural stone, unsealed timber or another delicate finish, provide the product instructions before work begins. Unknown chemicals should not be tested on an entire surface.

## Carpet and hard floors come last

Vacuuming and mopping should be the final major tasks after dusting, cupboards and wet areas are complete. Limit movement until hard floors are dry.

Ordinary vacuuming does not replace [Carpet Steam Cleaning](/services/carpet-clean/). If carpets need treatment, disclose room quantities, stairs, stains, fibres, furniture status and drying access. Arrange enough ventilation and drying time before beds, boxes or rugs cover the carpet.

For hard floors, match the method to the surface. Cleaning cannot reverse worn coatings, scratches, water damage or permanent staining.

## A room-by-room move-in checklist

| Area | Priority before unpacking |
| --- | --- |
| Kitchen | Empty cabinets, food-contact surfaces, appliances and floors |
| Bathrooms | Fixtures, screens, cupboards, contact points and floors |
| Bedrooms | Wardrobes, ledges, skirting and accessible floors |
| Living areas | Dust, glass selected in scope, doors and floors |
| Laundry | Trough, storage, fittings and floor |
| Entry | Door, handles, visible ledges and the final clean pathway |

Keep one essentials box separate from the main move. Include hand soap, toilet paper, towels, chargers, medication, basic food items and clean bedding so the household can function without opening every box on the first evening.

## Move-in cleaning versus end-of-lease cleaning

Both services work best in an empty property, but their purpose differs. A move-in clean prepares the home for the incoming household. An end-of-lease clean supports the outgoing tenant's handover against the agreed scope and condition record.

If you are leaving one rental and entering another, do not assume a single booking can cover both addresses or that both properties need identical extras. Use the [Victorian end-of-lease checklist](/blog/end-of-lease-cleaning-checklist-victoria/) for the property you are returning.

## Final check before boxes arrive

Walk through after the property has dried. Check cupboards before filling them, confirm bathrooms and kitchen fixtures, photograph the completed condition and keep movers away from wet floors.

If an included cleaning item appears to have been missed, report it promptly with clear photographs. When the property is ready, [book the move-in clean](/booking/) with enough time between access and the arrival of furniture.
