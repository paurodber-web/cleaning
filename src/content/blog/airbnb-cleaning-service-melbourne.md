## What is included in an Airbnb turnover clean?

An Airbnb turnover clean resets an entire short-stay property between confirmed checkout and the next check-in. The core work normally includes bathrooms, kitchen surfaces and appliances, dusting, floors, bins, fresh linen, guest supplies and a final presentation check. The exact scope should match the amenities and promises shown in the listing.

Unlike an ordinary [Standard House Clean](/services/standard-clean/), a turnover has no comfortable second chance after the guest opens the door. A missing towel, used item in a drawer or hair on clean bedding can affect trust even when the rest of the property looks good.

Airbnb's current host guidance says listed spaces should be clean and free of health hazards at check-in. It also recommends a repeatable strategy, enough time between bookings and a backup option if the usual cleaner is unavailable. Hosts should review Airbnb's [official cleaning guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/ensuring-your-place-is-sparkling-clean-570) alongside local requirements and their own property rules.

## Build one checklist for the specific property

A generic list is a useful base, but every Melbourne short-stay needs its own operating checklist. Record the number of beds, linen sizes, approved products, supply locations, appliance instructions, bin arrangements, parking access and the exact presentation expected in each room.

Keep permanent property information separate from booking-specific notes. The cleaner should be able to see quickly whether the next reservation needs a cot, sofa bed, extra towels or a different bed configuration.

## Start with access, ventilation and laundry

Confirm that the previous guest has left before entering and that the property is safe to access. Open windows where weather, security and building rules allow. Walk through once before cleaning so maintenance issues, excessive mess or missing stock can be reported early.

Strip every used bed and collect towels before starting detailed work. Laundry often controls the whole turnover timeline, so begin the first load immediately. If linen is processed off-site, define who collects, delivers and checks it. Never assume that a bag of linen is clean simply because it is folded.

Keep at least one complete backup set for every bed configuration. Same-day bookings leave little time to solve a stained sheet, broken zip or delayed dryer.

## Kitchen turnover checklist

Remove leftover food according to the host's policy and check the fridge, freezer, oven and microwave. Clean and inspect:

- benchtops, splashback, sink and tapware;
- cooktop, oven and visible appliance surfaces;
- fridge shelves and drawers when part of the turnover scope;
- crockery, cookware, glassware and utensils used by guests;
- cupboard fronts, handles and high-touch points;
- dining table, chairs and floors;
- bins, liners and the area around the bin.

Count essential items if shortages repeatedly cause problems. A simple minimum-stock list for dishwasher tablets, bin liners, tea, coffee and paper products is easier to verify than a vague instruction to restock the kitchen.

## Bathroom and bedroom turnover checklist

Bathrooms need a deliberate hair and residue check after surfaces have dried. Clean the toilet, shower or bath, screen, basin, vanity, mirrors, taps, floors and accessible fittings. Refill only the products agreed with the host, wipe containers and position towels consistently.

In bedrooms, inspect the mattress protector and pillows before fitting clean linen. Make the bed to the listing standard, remove lint or hair, dust reachable surfaces and check under the bed where accessible. Confirm that lamps, chargers, remotes and visible storage are ready for use.

Fresh linen should be dry, unstained and neutral-smelling. Strong fragrance can mask a problem and may be uncomfortable for guests; cleanliness should be verifiable without perfume.

## Restocking needs its own inventory

Restocking is operational work, not an invisible part of cleaning. Decide who purchases supplies, where stock is stored, what minimum level triggers a message and whether the cleaner is authorised to replace an item.

A useful inventory can include:

| Area | Typical items to verify |
| --- | --- |
| Bathroom | Toilet paper, hand soap, body wash, shampoo, clean towels |
| Kitchen | Dish liquid, dishwasher tablets, bin liners, tea and coffee |
| Bedroom | Complete linen set, spare blanket, hangers |
| Property | Batteries, light bulbs and any listing-specific welcome items |

Do not advertise amenities that the turnover process cannot supply consistently. Update the checklist whenever the listing changes.

## Plan same-day changeovers backwards

Work backwards from the promised check-in time. Allow time for access, laundry, cleaning, bed making, restocking, a quality check and reporting. Then add a buffer for heavier-than-normal use, lift delays, parking or a maintenance issue.

If the available window cannot hold the complete scope, change the operating plan before accepting another tight booking. Options include a second linen set, off-site laundry, a larger team, a later check-in or a blocked night between stays. An [Hourly Cleaning](/services/hourly-clean/) arrangement can suit a clearly prioritised property, but the booked team hours still need to cover the repeatable turnover standard.

## Complete a guest-view quality check

The person who cleaned a room can become visually familiar with it. A final walkthrough should therefore follow a fixed route and view the property as a new guest would: entrance first, then lighting, smell, beds, bathrooms, kitchen, floors and amenities.

Check that:

- the entrance is clean and access instructions work;
- every bed has the correct linen and guest count;
- no hair remains on bedding, bathroom surfaces or floors;
- dishes and appliances are clean and ready to use;
- bins are empty and fresh liners fitted;
- supplies match the agreed minimum;
- windows, heating or cooling are left as instructed;
- maintenance or damage has been reported with useful photographs.

## How much does Airbnb cleaning cost in Melbourne?

Turnover pricing depends on property size, bathrooms, bed configurations, laundry, restocking, parking and the time available between stays. A domestic-clean headline price may not include linen handling, inventory checks or guest-ready presentation.

Ask for a written scope and clarify whether the quote covers laundry, supplied products, consumable purchasing, balcony work, inside appliances and reporting. Compare the scope with the broader [Melbourne house cleaning price guide](/blog/how-much-does-house-cleaning-cost-in-melbourne/) and use the [live price calculator](/pricing/#calculator) for ordinary services. Short-stay properties with custom operating requirements should be assessed directly.

## Before arranging recurring turnovers

Prepare one test turnover before relying on the process during a tight same-day changeover. Walk the property with the checklist, time the real tasks and correct missing instructions. Share listing changes, blocked dates and maintenance work early.
