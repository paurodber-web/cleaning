## What is included in the core price?

The core service is designed for an empty rental. It covers accessible kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, laundry surfaces and floors according to the published checklist. For Maid At Home, the **inside oven and empty, accessible cabinet interiors are already included** in the end-of-lease price.

Typical core work includes:

- kitchen benches, splashback, sink and tapware;
- accessible cooktop, oven interior, racks and door glass;
- empty cupboard and drawer interiors and exteriors;
- shower, bath, toilet, vanity, mirrors and bathroom floors;
- accessible surfaces, ledges, skirting and wardrobes;
- vacuuming and mopping suitable floors;
- reachable cobwebs, fittings and visible marks within scope.

Cleaning does not repair damage, replace worn sealant, remediate mould, remove hazardous material or restore permanently stained surfaces. Compare the detailed [Victorian end-of-lease cleaning checklist](/blog/end-of-lease-cleaning-checklist-victoria/) before relying on a headline price.

## Why does end-of-lease cleaning cost more?

Routine cleaning maintains an occupied home. End-of-lease cleaning works through an empty-property handover scope that includes internal storage and appliance detail normally excluded from a standard visit. It also needs enough time for the customer to inspect the result before returning keys.

The price reflects:

- a broader room-by-room checklist;
- oven and empty-storage interiors;
- detail around edges, ledges and handover areas;
- coordination of empty access, keys and parking;
- higher risk from rushed moving timelines;
- optional specialist work such as carpet steam cleaning.

A low quote is not cheaper if it excludes tasks the property actually needs. Compare like with like rather than comparing one complete scope with another provider's starting price.

## What Victorian renters are actually required to do

Consumer Affairs Victoria says a renter must leave the property **reasonably clean** and in the same condition as at the beginning of the rental agreement, allowing for fair wear and tear. For agreements signed from 29 March 2021, a rental provider cannot automatically require professional cleaning in every case. It may be required where the property must be returned to its starting professionally cleaned state or where its condition requires professional cleaning.

Read the current [Consumer Affairs Victoria condition-report guidance](https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/housing/renting/rent-bond-bills-and-condition-reports/condition-reports) and your own rental agreement. This price guide is cleaning information, not legal advice, and no cleaner can promise a particular bond decision.

## DIY versus professional cleaning cost

DIY can cost less in cash when the property is maintained, the tenant has enough time and all required equipment is available. It can cost more in time if the work competes with packing, removalists, utility deadlines and key return.

Estimate the complete DIY requirement before deciding:

- cleaning products and safe equipment;
- oven and storage time;
- carpet equipment or professional receipts if required;
- transport and rubbish disposal;
- enough hours for a daylight inspection;
- time to correct missed cleaning before key handover.

Professional cleaning is most valuable when the deadline is fixed, the property is larger or the customer wants a defined service scope. It remains the renter's responsibility to compare the completed property with the entry condition report.

## How to compare end-of-lease quotes

Ask every provider the same questions:

1. Is GST included?
2. Is the oven interior included?
3. Are empty cupboards and drawers included?
4. Is carpet steam cleaning separate?
5. Are interior windows, blinds, walls or balconies extra?
6. What property configuration does the price assume?
7. What condition can change the quote?
8. How quickly must cleaning concerns be reported?
9. What access, parking and utilities are required?
10. Is any claim about a bond limited to cleaning issues within the booked scope?

A written checklist is more useful than broad language such as "full bond clean".

## How to keep the final price predictable

Finish moving first, remove rubbish, keep electricity and hot water connected and supply the agent's written checklist before the booking. Confirm keys, fobs, lifts and legal parking. If the property has heavy grease, mould concerns, delicate finishes or extra rooms, disclose them with current photographs.

Book the clean before key handover, not at the final available hour. Leave enough time to inspect and report an included missed item. Maid At Home asks customers to raise agreed cleaning concerns within 48 hours so the service can be reviewed.
