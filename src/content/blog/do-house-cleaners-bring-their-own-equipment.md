## What do house cleaners normally bring?

There is no universal rule across Melbourne cleaning companies. Some cleaners arrive with products, cloths, a vacuum and a mop. Others supply products and small tools but use the customer's floor equipment. The correct answer is the arrangement stated by the provider and confirmed for your booking.

For Maid At Home, cleaners can bring professional cleaning products. Where possible, the customer provides a safe, working vacuum and mop suited to the home's floors. If that is not possible, tell the team before booking so an appropriately equipped cleaner can be organised, subject to availability.

This distinction matters because an assignment made on the assumption that equipment is available may not include transport space for a full vacuum and mop. Confirming it early protects usable cleaning time.

## What should you provide?

Provide safe access to running water, electricity and suitable lighting. If you supply the vacuum or mop, check that it works, is reasonably clean and has the attachments needed for the property.

Useful items include:

- a vacuum suitable for the home's carpet and hard floors;
- a mop appropriate for timber, tile or other finishes;
- access to a sink and water;
- any product you specifically require the cleaner to use;
- care instructions for delicate materials.

You do not need to buy a collection of specialist chemicals. If you prefer your own products because of fragrance, allergies or a sensitive finish, mention this when booking and leave them in their original labelled containers.

The [Maid At Home FAQs](/faqs/) explain the current arrangement. If the property has no usable vacuum or mop, [contact the team](/contact/) before selecting a service.

## Products, tools and equipment are different

Products include sprays, detergents and surface treatments. Small tools include cloths, dusters and brushes. Equipment usually means larger items such as a vacuum, mop system or carpet extraction machine.

A [Standard House Clean](/services/standard-clean/) includes routine vacuuming and mopping of accessible floors, not specialist machinery. Steam extraction requires the separate [Carpet Cleaning service](/services/carpet-clean/), with equipment, treatment time and drying guidance matched to that task.

Likewise, an oven interior is not included in every maintenance booking. Review the [house-cleaning inclusions guide](/blog/what-is-included-in-house-clean/) before comparing providers.

## Why using the home's vacuum can be practical

A machine already suited to the property can reduce the transfer of dust or pet hair between homes and is more likely to have the right attachment for a rug, staircase or floor finish.

That only helps when it is safe and functional. A vacuum with a blocked filter, damaged cable or missing head slows the work and may be unsuitable. If equipment fails before the visit, contact the team rather than waiting for the cleaner to discover it.

## When should the cleaner bring equipment?

Request an equipped cleaner when:

- the property has no usable vacuum or mop;
- you are moving into or out of an empty property;
- access is through an agent or concierge;
- existing equipment is damaged or unsuitable;
- a specialist service requires professional machinery.

Make the request before confirmation because it can affect which cleaner is assigned. For an empty-property handover, review [End of Lease Cleaning](/services/end-of-lease-clean/). For a defined priority list, [Hourly Cleaning](/services/hourly-clean/) may suit, but equipment expectations still need to be agreed.

## Allergies and delicate surfaces

Tell the provider about fragrance sensitivities, product restrictions, natural stone, unsealed timber, delicate metals or damaged finishes. A cleaner should not guess which chemical is safe for an unfamiliar material.

Do not ask anyone to mix chemicals or use an unlabelled product. Cleaning cannot repair a damaged coating or guarantee removal of permanent staining.

## Equipment checklist before booking

1. Check whether products are supplied.
2. Confirm who provides the vacuum and mop.
3. Test customer-supplied equipment.
4. Disclose pets, allergies and sensitive materials.
5. Request specialist equipment in advance.
6. Confirm that the selected service includes the expected task.

If you are deciding between service types, compare [Standard and Deep Cleaning](/blog/standard-vs-deep-cleaning/) or review the [online pricing page](/pricing/).

## Confirm the arrangement in writing

Do not assume professional cleaning always means a van full of equipment, and do not assume the customer supplies everything. Ask one specific question: who is bringing the products, vacuum and mop for this booking?

For Maid At Home, provide working floor equipment where possible or request an equipped cleaner before confirmation. That simple step keeps the appointment focused on cleaning.
