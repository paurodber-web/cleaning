## The realistic end-of-lease cleaning timeframe

Plan for most of a working day rather than treating an end-of-lease clean as a short maintenance visit. A small, empty and maintained apartment may finish sooner. A larger property, heavy buildup, difficult access or specialist extras can extend the work.

Team size matters. Two cleaners working for four hours represent eight team hours. Comparing clock time without team size can make quotes look different when the labour allowance is similar.

Ask for the planned arrival window, expected team size and scope. Do not treat a general guide as a guaranteed completion time for a specific home.

## What makes the clean take longer?

### Size, bathrooms and condition

More bedrooms normally mean more floors, ledges, doors and storage. Bathrooms and kitchens often require more detail than empty bedrooms, so two properties with the same bedroom count can need different time.

Recent dust differs from thick grease, heavy soap residue, widespread wall marks or long-neglected detail. Send photos when the condition is difficult to describe.

### Furniture and belongings

End-of-lease cleaning is designed for an empty property. Boxes, furniture and rubbish block floors, cabinet interiors and edges. Removal, packing and disposal should be completed first.

### Access and specialist work

Key collection, lift bookings, loading restrictions and distant parking can delay the start. Carpet steam cleaning, windows and selected extras add treatment time. The [carpet-cleaning timing guide](/blog/how-long-does-carpet-cleaning-take-melbourne/) explains why treatment and drying are separate.

## A practical booking timeline

### Several days before

Choose the service, send accurate property details and confirm access. Book removalists before cleaners so the property is empty. Arrange any carpet work and review the tenancy agreement and condition report.

### The day before

Remove belongings and rubbish, empty cabinets and appliances, defrost the freezer if relevant, secure valuables and confirm electricity and water remain connected.

### Cleaning day

Provide access at the agreed time and keep the property clear. Avoid scheduling key return immediately after the expected finish. Delays, condition differences or drying time can otherwise create unnecessary pressure.

### After the clean

Inspect in daylight where possible, compare the property with the agreed scope and take dated photographs. Maid At Home asks customers to report an eligible cleaning concern within 48 hours so the booking can be reviewed.

## Should carpet cleaning happen on the same day?

It can, but sequencing matters. General cleaning should avoid re-soiling treated carpet, and steam-cleaned fibres need ventilation and restricted foot traffic while drying.

Tell the team how many carpeted rooms and stairs need treatment. Allow approximately 6–12 hours for typical drying, depending on ventilation, humidity, fibre and treatment conditions. Do not replace furniture before the carpet is dry.

## How long before key handover?

Leave enough time for:

1. completion of the agreed scope;
2. carpet drying where applicable;
3. your inspection and photographs;
4. communication about an eligible missed item;
5. agent access and key return.

Booking at the final available hour removes this buffer. When possible, clean before the last tenancy day while utilities and lawful access remain available.

## DIY versus professional timing

DIY may work for a maintained small property when you have equipment, transport and uninterrupted time. It becomes harder when cleaning competes with packing, removalists, children, work and a fixed handover.

A professional service provides organised labour and an agreed scope, but cannot correct poor preparation or guarantee every form of damage is cleanable. Compare cost and time with the [Melbourne end-of-lease price guide](/blog/end-of-lease-cleaning-cost-melbourne/).

## How to get a more accurate estimate

Provide:

- bedroom and bathroom count;
- property type and levels;
- whether the home will be empty;
- current-condition photographs;
- oven, cabinet, window and carpet requirements;
- parking, lift and key instructions;
- inspection and key-return deadlines.

## Build in time to inspect

The safest answer is a realistic sequence: empty the property, complete the agreed clean, allow specialist work to dry, inspect the result and keep a buffer before handover.
