## Home size and condition

A larger home normally needs more cleaning time, but condition matters just as much as the number of rooms. A maintained two-bedroom home can require less work than a smaller property with significant buildup.

When requesting a quote, describe the current condition honestly and mention any areas that need special attention. This helps the cleaner recommend a realistic service.

## Hourly and flat-rate cleaning

Hourly cleaning works well when you have a priority list and want the cleaner to focus on selected tasks within an agreed amount of time. Flat-rate services are useful when you want a defined checklist and a clearer total before the booking.

Compare what is included rather than looking only at the headline price. Equipment, cleaning products, insurance and the scope of work can differ between providers.

## Regular and deep cleaning

A [Standard House Clean](/services/standard-clean/) is intended for maintained homes and can be booked once or on a recurring schedule. A [Deep Clean](/services/deep-clean/) allows more time for detail work and areas with accumulated dust, grease or soap residue.

Choosing the correct service from the beginning reduces the chance of an unrealistic booking or unexpected additions on the day.

## How recurring cleaning savings work

Eligible Standard, Deep and Hourly services receive **10% off weekly or fortnightly cleaning** and **5% off cleaning every three or four weeks**. The saving begins with the second service; the first clean is charged at the normal rate because it establishes the home's starting condition and service baseline.

A discount should not decide the frequency by itself. Weekly cleaning is useful where floors, kitchens and bathrooms become heavily used within days. Fortnightly is a practical middle ground. Three- or four-week visits work better when occupants actively maintain the home between professional cleans.

## Costs that may sit outside the quote

A clear estimate should identify which extras are included and what may change the scope. Common reasons to contact the provider before booking include:

- more living rooms, studies, bathrooms or levels than the standard configuration;
- heavy grease, soap residue, mould concerns or long-term neglect;
- paid parking, difficult access or building loading restrictions;
- delicate finishes requiring agreed products;
- rubbish removal, hazardous material or unsafe-height work;
- carpet steam cleaning, exterior windows or other specialist work.

The cheapest headline figure is not cheaper if it excludes the tasks you actually need. Compare like with like: the same rooms, condition, extras, equipment expectations and follow-up terms.

## What to ask before booking

Ask whether the price includes GST, how team hours are counted, which products or equipment must be available, when recurring savings start and what happens if an agreed item is missed. For Maid At Home, service issues should be reported within 48 hours so the scope can be reviewed and the appropriate follow-up coordinated.
